## About

I build and ship end-to-end products — native iOS apps in SwiftUI and full-stack web apps in Next.js. Not prototypes: apps with real users, App Store releases, payments, cloud sync, and production deploys.

Most of my work starts with a problem I actually have. **The BitBinder** exists because stand-up comics keep their material in a mess of Notes files and voice memos. **The Trip Handler** exists because organizing a group trip over group chat is chaos. I find the friction, then build the thing that removes it.

- 🚀 **Shipped to the App Store** — [The BitBinder](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-bitbinder/id6756085897), a SwiftUI app with SwiftData + CloudKit sync, on-device speech recognition, and AI-assisted workflows
- 🧱 **Full-stack, not just front-end** — auth, data modeling, Stripe payments, invite/permission flows, deploys
- 🔐 **Care about the details** — client-side encryption, offline-first sync, on-device ML over sending data to a server when I can avoid it
- 🎬 **Domain knowledge that shows** — live production, comedy, and film, which is why the tools I build for those spaces actually fit the workflow

## Featured Work

### 📓 [The BitBinder](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/The-Bit-Binder) · <sub>[**Live on the App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-bitbinder/id6756085897)</sub>
A native SwiftUI app for stand-up comics to capture, organize, record, transcribe, and refine material — the whole lifecycle of a joke in one place.
<br><sub>`SwiftUI` · `SwiftData` · `CloudKit` · `AVFoundation` · `Speech` · `Vision` · `PDFKit` · on-device ML + OpenAI</sub>

### 🎤 [Showrunner — I Can Run A Show](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/Showrunner-ICanRunAShow) · <sub>[icanrunashow.com](https://www.icanrunashow.com)</sub>
The full live-show workflow in one app: plan the lineup, import a schedule straight from a photo, then run the show in full-screen live mode with cue timers and walk-on music.
<br><sub>`React` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · PWA · client-side encryption</sub>

### 🧳 [The Trip Handler](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/the-trip-handler) · <sub>[live demo](https://the-trip-handler.vercel.app)</sub>
Full-stack group-trip organizer: create a trip, share a private invite link or join code, collect and approve applications, then plan lodging, meals, itinerary, contributions, expenses, and per-person Stripe payments.
<br><sub>`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Stripe` · full auth + permissions</sub>

### 🎬 [RoleCall](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/Role-Call)
Production-ready planning app for filmmakers — crew roles, cast, script, shot list, and schedule — so nothing falls through the cracks on shoot day. Built for everyone from Reels creators to feature directors.
<br><sub>`Next.js` · `TypeScript`</sub>

### 📹 [VlogNudge](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/vlognudge)
An ADHD-aware iOS app that nudges you to film day-in-the-life clips. Your day, one clip at a time — designed around how attention actually works, not how it's supposed to.
<br><sub>`SwiftUI` · notifications · video capture</sub>

### 💸 [BillSpilt](https://github.com/taylordrew4u2/Bill-Spilt) · <sub>[billspilt.com](https://billspilt.com)</sub>
Roommate bill splitting, made painless.
<br><sub>`TypeScript` · `Next.js`</sub>
